## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract backdrop for a software interface: deep water at night in dark
blue-teal (#0a1a22) filling almost the whole frame. Along the bottom edge only, gentle, low
wave bands a shade lighter and darker than the field, with the faintest aqua (#4fd1c5) light
on their crests. The upper two thirds are plain, still and dark. Subtle grain, calm, empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, boats, fish, a moon or its reflection, bright areas, foam,
sharp detail, anything lighter than dark teal outside the crest lines.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the tidal dark Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
