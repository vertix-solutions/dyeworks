## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a single scallop shell seen from the front, its
fan in sea-glass teal (#2aa8a0) with five ribs and a rim in deep teal (#0b6e78), and a small
hinge in bright aqua (#4fd1c5). Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like
edges, flat fills. Must read on both a pale and a very dark ground.

Text, letters, a background, a frame, sand, water, sparkles, gradients, more than three
colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
