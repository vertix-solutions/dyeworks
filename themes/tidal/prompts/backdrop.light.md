## Prompt
A wide, very pale abstract backdrop for a light software interface: the colour of frosted sea
glass (#eef6f5) filling almost the whole frame. Along the bottom edge only, three gentle,
low wave bands in slightly deeper sea-glass tints (#e2efed to #d5e7e4), soft as a watercolour
wash, with the faintest teal crest lines. The upper two thirds are plain and calm. Fine paper
grain, soft, empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, boats, birds, shells, rocks, a beach, a sun, foam spray, strong
contrast, any area darker than pale sea glass.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the tidal light Tokens (a light Scheme: the darkest 0.1 % of pixels is
bounded and the image is lightened towards --bg).
