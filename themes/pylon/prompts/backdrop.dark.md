## Prompt
A wide, dark abstract desert-at-dusk backdrop for a software interface. Rolling dunes of dark
ochre sand under a deep indigo night sky (#171224), the horizon low, the whole scene dim and
quiet. A handful of small faceted blue crystals (#4fc3ff) float above the dunes, lit faintly
from within, each pooling a soft cerulean light on the sand directly beneath it; they are the
only light in the scene and they are small. Fine grain, painterly, mostly empty, darker towards
the centre so content can sit on it.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, buildings, ruins, books, roads, vegetation, water, a
moon, stars dense enough to read as a pattern, lens flare, large or bright crystals, any area
lighter than dim sand outside the crystals.
