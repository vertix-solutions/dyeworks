## Prompt
A wide, dark abstract desert-at-dusk backdrop for a software interface. Rolling dunes of dark
ochre sand under a deep indigo night sky (#171224), the horizon low, the whole scene dim and
quiet. A handful of small faceted blue crystals (#4fc3ff) float above the dunes only at the far
left and right edges of the frame, inside a narrow strip at each edge, lit faintly from within,
each pooling a soft cerulean light on the sand directly beneath it; they are the only light in
the scene and they are small. The centre of the frame, between the two strips and from top to
bottom, is plain dune and sky with no crystal and no pool of light. Fine grain, painterly,
mostly empty, darker towards the centre so content can sit on it.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, buildings, ruins, books, roads, vegetation, water, a
moon, stars dense enough to read as a pattern, lens flare, large or bright crystals, any area
lighter than dim sand outside the crystals, a crystal or a pool of light anywhere between the two
edge strips.
