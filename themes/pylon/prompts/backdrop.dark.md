## Prompt
A wide, dark abstract desert-at-dusk backdrop for a software interface. Rolling dunes of dark
ochre sand under a deep indigo night sky (#171224), the horizon low, the whole scene dim and
quiet. A faint cerulean haze (#4fc3ff) high at the upper right is the only light in the scene.
Only dunes, sky and haze from edge to edge, with no object anywhere in the frame. Fine grain,
painterly, empty, darker towards the centre so content can sit on it.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, buildings, ruins, books, roads, vegetation, water, a
moon, stars dense enough to read as a pattern, lens flare, crystals, shadows, pools of light,
any object on or above the dunes, any area lighter than dim sand outside the haze.
