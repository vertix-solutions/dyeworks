## Prompt
A minimal illustration on a transparent background for an empty list: one low dune of warm
ochre sand (#c9a46a) across the lower third, its edges fading to transparent, and above its
crest a single small faceted blue crystal (facets in #1860ad, #4fc3ff and #9fe0ff) floating
with one sharp lapis shadow on the sand beneath it. Flat painterly fills, lots of empty space,
nothing else in the frame.

## Must not
Text, letters, numbers, people, animals, buildings, ruins, books, a sky, a horizon line, a sun
or moon, stars, glow, more than four colours.

## Accept
≥ 50 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
