## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a single faceted blue crystal, an elongated
hexagon pointed at both ends, floating upright above a small mound of sand. The crystal shows
three facets in deep lapis (#1860ad), cerulean (#4fc3ff) and pale ice (#9fe0ff); the mound is
warm ochre (#c9a46a). A thin sharp lapis shadow lies on the mound directly under the crystal.
Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like edges, flat fills, no outer glow.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, a horizon line, sparkles, grains floating around the
crystal, gradients other than the three facets, more than four colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
