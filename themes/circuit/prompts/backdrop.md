## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract backdrop for a software interface, evoking a printed circuit board
and a neural network. Near-black green-tinted surface (#060a09). A faint regular grid, and a
few thin teal (#19f0a8) traces running at right angles with small round pads at their ends,
all at very low brightness, denser in the top right corner and fading to nothing elsewhere.
Precise, quiet, technical.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, chips with legible markings, bright lines, glow blooms, blue or
purple tones, any area lighter than dark teal.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the circuit Tokens.
