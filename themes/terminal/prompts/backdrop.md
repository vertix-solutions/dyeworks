## Prompt
A wide, very dark backdrop for a software interface that looks like the glass of an old
phosphor CRT monitor switched on but showing nothing. Near-black screen with a faint green
cast (#050a06) filling the whole frame, very fine horizontal scanlines barely visible across
it, a soft dim green phosphor glow at the centre and a vignette darkening every corner.
Subtle glass grain. Calm, empty, atmospheric.

## Must not
Text, letters, numbers, characters, code, cursors, logos, a monitor bezel, a keyboard, a desk,
people, bright areas, lens flare, moiré, anything lighter than a dark green outside the centre
glow.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the terminal Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
