## Prompt
A wide, very dark backdrop for a software interface: a polar night sky in deep navy (#0a1020)
filling almost the whole frame. Along the very top edge only, faint curtains of aurora light
in green (#5cf2b4) with violet (#b28cff) hems, soft and diffuse like light on thin cloud,
fading out well above the middle of the frame. A few sparse, dim stars. Subtle grain. Calm,
atmospheric, mostly empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, trees, mountains, buildings, a moon, bright areas,
lens flare, sharp detail, anything lighter than dark navy below the top third.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the aurora Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
