## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a single curved ribbon of aurora light running
from green (#5cf2b4) to violet (#b28cff), arched over a small four-pointed white (#e8f1ff)
star with a thin dark navy (#0a1020) outline. Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean
vector-like edges, flat fills apart from the ribbon's two-colour blend.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, mountains, a moon, sparkles around the star, more than
four colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
