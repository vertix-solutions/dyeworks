## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a single mint leaf on a short stem, tilted up and to
the right, its lower half deep mint green (#0f7a5a) and its upper half bright mint (#6fe3c1), with a
thin pale midrib. Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, rounded friendly shape, clean
vector-like edges, flat fills.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, more than one leaf, water drops, sparkles, gradients other
than the two halves, more than three colours.

## Accept
≥ 40 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
