## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a synthwave setting sun, a hot magenta
(#ff2bd6) disc crossed by three thin horizontal gaps, with a faint cyan (#00e5ff) outer
glow. Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like edges, no gradient other
than the glow.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, a horizon line, palm trees, more than two colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
