## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract synthwave backdrop for a software interface. Deep navy night
(#0a0d1f) filling almost the whole frame. Low on the horizon, right of centre, a faint
magenta and violet haze. A sparse perspective grid of thin cyan lines on the floor, barely
visible, fading into darkness. Subtle film grain. Soft, calm, atmospheric, mostly empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, a sun disc, mountains, cars, people, bright areas, lens flare,
sharp detail in the upper two thirds, anything lighter than a dark navy outside the haze.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the neon Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
