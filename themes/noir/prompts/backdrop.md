## Prompt
A wide, very dark black-and-white film-noir backdrop for a software interface: an empty
dark room (#0b0b0b) filling the whole frame, with faint diagonal slats of light from a
venetian blind falling only across the top right corner and fading out well before the
centre. Heavy film grain, deep vignette, almost all of the frame near black. Calm and
empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, silhouettes, furniture, windows, a blind itself, cigarettes,
smoke, red or any colour, bright areas, light slats crossing the centre, anything lighter
than a dark grey outside the slats.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the noir Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
