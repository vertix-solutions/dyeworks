## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract backdrop for a software interface, like the inside of a forge
after the fire has died down: warm charcoal (#14100e) filling almost the whole frame, a
faint dark orange-brown haze rising from the very bottom edge, and a few tiny dim golden
specks drifting just above it. Smoky, soft, calm, mostly empty; the upper two thirds near
black.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, flames, fire, an anvil, tools, people, bright areas, glowing coals in
the frame, sparks in the upper half, lens flare, anything lighter than a dark brown outside
the haze.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the ember Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
