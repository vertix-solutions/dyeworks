## Prompt
A wide, very pale abstract dawn backdrop for a light software interface. A warm off-white field
(#fff8f2) filling almost the whole frame, with a soft apricot glow (#ffe6cf to #ffd9c4) rising
from the bottom edge only, as if the sun were about to come up just below the frame. Smooth
gradient, faint paper grain, calm and empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, a sun disc, clouds with hard edges, mountains, buildings, people, birds,
saturated orange, strong contrast, any area darker than pale apricot, any detail in the upper half.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the sunrise Tokens (a light Scheme: the darkest 0.1 % of pixels is
bounded and the image is lightened towards --bg).
