## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a half sun rising over a straight horizon line.
The half disc is warm amber (#e08a1c); five short rays and the horizon line are coral red
(#b83c25). Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like edges, flat fills.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, clouds, water, gradients, a face on the sun, more than two
colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
