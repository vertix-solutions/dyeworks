## Prompt
A wide, very pale abstract backdrop for a light software interface in a cherry blossom mood.
A blush white field (#fdf6f8) filling almost the whole frame, with a faint soft-focus branch
silhouette reaching in from the top right corner and a few pale pink petals (#f4dbe5) drifting
along the edges. The middle of the frame stays bare. Watercolour softness, low contrast, calm.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, buildings, a sun, full blossoms in the middle of the frame,
saturated pink, strong contrast, any area darker than pale blush, any detail in the centre.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the sakura light Tokens (a light Scheme: the darkest 0.1 % of pixels is
bounded and the image is lightened towards --bg).
