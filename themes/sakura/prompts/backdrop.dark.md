## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract backdrop for a dark software interface on a cherry blossom night.
A deep plum field (#1c1219) filling almost the whole frame, with a barely visible branch
silhouette reaching in from the top right corner and a few dim petals (#33202d) drifting along
the edges. The middle of the frame stays bare. Soft focus, subtle grain, calm and quiet.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, buildings, a moon, lanterns, bright petals, glowing areas,
anything lighter than a dark plum, any detail in the centre.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the sakura dark Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
