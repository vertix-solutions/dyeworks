## Prompt
A wide, very pale risograph-printed backdrop for a light software interface. Cool off-white
uncoated paper (#f4f4ef) filling almost the whole frame, with a faint halftone dot field fading
in from the top left corner in pale fluorescent pink (#f0d6e1) and another from the bottom right
corner in pale print blue (#d8e1f2), the dots shrinking toward the bare middle. Slight paper
grain and uneven ink, flat and calm.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, objects, illustrations, saturated ink, dots in the middle of the
frame, strong contrast, any area darker than pale paper.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the riso Tokens (a light Scheme: the darkest 0.1 % of pixels is bounded
and the image is lightened towards --bg).
