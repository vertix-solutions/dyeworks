## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background in a risograph print style: two overlapping
circles side by side, the left in fluorescent pink (#c0185f) and the right in print blue
(#1f56c8), their overlap the dark violet (#3a1a5e) the two inks make when overprinted. Slight
grain in the ink, centred, filling 70 percent of the frame.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, outlines, gradients, more than three colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
