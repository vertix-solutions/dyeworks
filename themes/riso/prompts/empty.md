## Prompt
A minimal illustration on a transparent background for an empty list in a risograph print style:
one empty rectangular frame printed twice, once in print blue (#1f56c8) and once in fluorescent
pink (#c0185f), the two layers slightly out of register, nothing inside the frame. Thick flat
strokes with a little ink grain, lots of empty space.

## Must not
Text, letters, numbers, people, objects inside the frame, a background, fills, more than two
colours.

## Accept
≥ 50 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
