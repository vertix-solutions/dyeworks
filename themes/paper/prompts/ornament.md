## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a single fountain-pen nib pointing straight down,
in deep ink blue (#2b4c7e) with its right half a slightly lighter blue (#3d649e), a small round
breather hole and a fine slit running to the point, and a narrow near-black collar at the top.
Centred, filling 70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like edges, flat fills.

## Must not
Text, letters, a background, a frame, a pen barrel, ink drops, splatter, sparkles, gradients,
more than four colours.

## Accept
≥ 40 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
