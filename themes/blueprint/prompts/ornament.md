## Prompt
A small flat icon on a transparent background: a drafting compass seen from the front,
two thin white (#ffffff) legs hinged at a round pale cyan (#9fe3ff) head, the legs open
about 40 degrees, with a short dashed pale cyan arc between their points. Centred, filling
70 percent of the frame, clean vector-like edges, flat fills.

Text, letters, a background, a frame, paper, a ruler, more than two colours.

## Accept
≥ 30 % of pixels fully transparent; all four corners transparent.
