## Prompt
A wide, dark abstract cyanotype backdrop for a software interface: a sheet of deep
blueprint blue paper (#0b2a4a) filling the whole frame, with a faint drafting grid of thin
white lines, fine minor squares and heavier major squares, both barely visible. The print is
slightly unevenly exposed, a little darker at the edges, with soft paper grain. Calm, flat,
mostly empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, numbers, dimension labels, a drawing of any object, machines, buildings,
plans, title blocks, logos, bright areas, white patches, folds or creases with strong
contrast.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the blueprint Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
