## Prompt
A wide, very pale abstract backdrop for a light software interface: soft lichen grey-green
(#f3f5ee) filling almost the whole frame, like a sheet of handmade paper. Along the bottom edge
only, low, soft mounds of moss and undergrowth in slightly deeper pale greens (#e9ede1 to
#dfe4d4), blurred and out of focus, with the faintest fern tips. The upper two thirds are plain
and calm. Fine paper grain, soft, empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, mushrooms, flowers, tree trunks, a path, sunbeams,
strong contrast, any area darker than pale lichen.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the moss light Tokens (a light Scheme: the darkest 0.1 % of pixels is
bounded and the image is lightened towards --bg).
