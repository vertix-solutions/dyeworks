## Prompt
A wide, very dark abstract backdrop for a software interface: the forest floor under a closed
canopy at dusk, a deep green-black (#12160f) filling almost the whole frame. Along the bottom
edge only, low soft mounds of moss and undergrowth barely lighter than the field, with the
faintest touches of moss green (#8fcf6a) on a few frond tips. The upper two thirds are plain
and dark. Subtle grain, calm, empty.

## Must not
Text, letters, logos, people, animals, mushrooms, fireflies, tree trunks, a moon, sunbeams,
bright areas, sharp detail, anything lighter than dark moss outside the frond tips.

## Accept
luminance clamp against the moss dark Tokens; no region brighter than the clamp allows.
